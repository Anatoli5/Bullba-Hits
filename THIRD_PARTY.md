# Sources and licences

No files of the closed-source Armor Inspector, its WASM or its server responses are included in the product build. Vehicle models are extracted locally from the installed client package; the distributed ZIP does not contain them.

- **TagTools**, Skyth, MIT. The type/ITEM/packed-integer parsing in `mod/local_armor_inspector/havok.py` follows the structure described by that code, adapted to the WoT 2020 SDK sections and to Python 2.7/3. The reading code is rewritten; the converter/writer and external executables are not used. [Source](https://github.com/blueskythlikesclouds/TagTools/blob/master/TagTools.py). Full licence: `licenses/TagTools.txt`.
- **Smellyriver TankInspector**, Smellyriver.OSS, MIT, commit `e07c6e49d210fc45f57d76b66372725812036d1e`. The packed XML layout was checked against `IO/XmlDecoding/PackedSection.cs`; `mod/local_armor_inspector/packed_xml.py` implements the reader in Python. [Repository](https://github.com/smellyriver/tankinspector). Licence: `licenses/TankInspector.txt`.
- **Battle Hits**, Andrii Andrushchyshyn, MIT, commit `b7aec39188466816f0b2560196ad8fff75e31a44`. Used as a reference for event interception and ModsList registration. Its interface and binary data collector are not included. [Repository](https://github.com/wot-public-mods/battle-hits). Licence: `licenses/BattleHits.txt`.
- **ModsList 1.7.9** (`me.poliroid.modslistapi_1.7.9.wotmod`), Andrii Andruschyshyn, MIT. The hangar mods panel our entry is registered in; shipped unchanged next to our mod and installed only when the game has no copy. [Repository](https://github.com/wot-public-mods/mods-list). Licence: `licenses/ModsList.txt`; SHA-256 is pinned in `tools/third_party.py`.
- **OpenWG Gameface 1.1.6** (`net.openwg.gameface_1.1.6.wotmod`), Andrii Andruschyshyn, MIT. Required by ModsList for its panel button; shipped unchanged under the same rule. [Repository](https://gitlab.com/openwg/wot.gameface). Licence: `licenses/OpenWGGameface.txt`; SHA-256 is pinned in `tools/third_party.py`.
- **Three.js 0.160.1**, MIT: `web/vendor/three.min.js`. [Release](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/releases/tag/r160). The licence is included as `web/vendor/three.LICENSE`; source and SHA-256 are in `web/vendor/manifest.json`.
- **three-mesh-bvh 0.8.3**, Garrett Johnson, MIT: `web/vendor/three-mesh-bvh.umd.js`. Bounding-volume tree of the vehicle model and its GLSL ray query, used by the penetration map to follow a shell after a ricochet. [Repository](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh). The licence is included as `web/vendor/three-mesh-bvh.LICENSE`; source and SHA-256 are in `web/vendor/manifest.json`.
- **Interface icons**, Wargaming.net: the 18 PNG files in `web/icons/` (equipment, crew perks and shell types) are taken from the installed World of Tanks client and ship with the page so that a fresh install is complete before the first game start. They are used unchanged in a free, non-commercial mod and will be removed at the rights holder’s request. No vehicle models, sounds or other client assets are distributed.
- **Python 3.14.7** is used locally for development and checks. It is not shipped and users do not need it.
- **OpenWG Python Compiler** is used only for building; the binary is not shipped. [openwg.build](https://gitlab.com/openwg/openwg.build), commit `d0704c7dbd062c861582c90a761a7e019af9b60a`; its SHA-256 is pinned in `tools/build.py`.
- **Inno Setup 7.1.0**, Jordan Russell / Martijn Laan, builds the EXE installer. [Official download](https://jrsoftware.org/isdl.php). The compiler is unpacked in portable mode into the working folder only; the product ships the standard setup stub. Licence: `licenses/InnoSetup.txt`; URL and SHA-256 of the official distribution: `work/installer-dependencies/source.json`.

The signatures of `Vehicle.showDamageFromShot`, `DamageFromShotDecoder`, `ModelHitTester` and `IBrowserController` were checked against the [client sources #935](https://github.com/izeberg/wot-src/tree/6cdd11a8ce3eae78b9a8a95c592523e9d0774a9a/sources/res/scripts) and the local #936 resources. Client sources are not included in the build. They are compatibility references, not a public stable Wargaming SDK.
